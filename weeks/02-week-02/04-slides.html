1. Standalone VR: Meta Quest 3 / 3S

  • Pancake lenses (Quest 3) vs Fresnel lenses (Quest 3S)
  • Full-colour passthrough + depth sensing
  • Inside-out tracked controllers
  • Camera-based hand tracking

Note

Standalone removed the PC-tether barrier — the single biggest driver of mass XR adoption.

2. Mixed Reality Headsets

Apple Vision Pro

  • “Spatial computing,” not VR/AR
  • visionOS: windows, volumes, spaces
  • Eye tracking + pinch input
  • Passthrough (video see-through)

HoloLens 2

  • Optical see-through waveguide
  • Narrow FOV, strong hand/gaze tracking
  • Enterprise/industrial focus

Passthrough vs Optical See-Through

Passthrough Optical See-Through
How Cameras + screen composite Transparent lens overlay
Examples Quest 3, Vision Pro HoloLens 2
Real-world fidelity Limited by cameras Perfect (direct light)
Latency risk Camera-to-display lag None for real world

3. AR Glasses: Xreal One Pro & Peers

  • Lightweight, glasses-form-factor, usually tethered
  • Birdbath/waveguide optics project a floating virtual screen
  • Some models add spatial/positional tracking for anchored content
  • Optimised for portability and casual wear, not immersion

AR Glasses vs Headsets

Factor AR Glasses MR/VR Headsets
Compute Tethered to host Onboard
FOV Narrow, screen-like Wide, immersive
Interaction Minimal Rich (hands, gaze, controllers)
Use case Screen replacement, media Games, training, spatial computing

5. A Framework for Critical Comparison

Four criteria to evaluate any XR device:

  • Comfort — weight, fit, session tolerance
  • Field of view (FOV) — degrees, edge clipping, task impact
  • Tracking fidelity — accuracy, latency, robustness
  • Ecosystem maturity — SDK, store, developer/content ecosystem

Summary

  • Standalone VR (Quest 3/3S), MR headsets (Vision Pro, HoloLens 2), and AR glasses (Xreal One Pro) represent three distinct hardware philosophies
  • Passthrough vs optical see-through is a key technical and experiential distinction
  • Platform choice (Horizon OS vs visionOS) shapes tooling, review, and distribution
  • Comfort, FOV, tracking fidelity, and ecosystem maturity give you a repeatable critique framework

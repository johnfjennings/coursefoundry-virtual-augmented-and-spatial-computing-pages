System Usability Scale Questionnaire

Printable instrument for the Week 6 lab peer test

Author

Mr. John Jennings

NoteHow to use this sheet

Print one copy per participant. Administer this after your peer has finished testing your recreated interaction (Week 6 Lab, Task 3.2). Ask them to respond based on their immediate impression — there are no right or wrong answers, and they should not think too long about any one item.

Participant ID / Initials: _______________________ Date: _______________________

Interaction/prototype tested: _________________________________________________

Instructions

For each statement, circle one number from 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree) that best reflects your immediate reaction.

1 = Strongly disagree 2 = Disagree 3 = Neutral 4 = Agree 5 = Strongly agree

Scoring Guide

The SUS score is calculated out of 100, but it is not a percentage — treat it as a relative score for comparison.

Step 1 — Convert raw scores:

  • For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): contribution = (circled score − 1)
  • For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): contribution = (5 − circled score)

TipInterpreting the score
  • Above 68 is considered above average usability
  • Below 51 suggests significant usability problems
  • A single score has limited meaning on its own — use it to compare your recreation against notes on the original experience, or against another classmate’s prototype

Notes

Use this space to record anything the participant said aloud while testing (think-aloud comments), hesitations, or errors observed — this qualitative detail is often more useful for your Assessment 2 evaluation than the numeric score alone.

Virtual, Augmented and Spatial Computing — all assessments and phases

Author

Mr. John Jennings

This is the checklist your tutor uses to sign off work for each session, each assessment phase and each milestone. Use it to check your own submissions before they are assessed.

Assessment 1 — Practical Evaluation, Continuous Lab Work (30%)

Rule: sign-off happens in the session, on the target device. Editor-only results do not evidence LO3. A portfolio entry with no signed-off deliverable behind it carries no marks.

On Moodle this component is graded as five separate assignments, PE-1 to PE-5, each marked out of 100 with simple direct grading. Evidence for every session also goes in the Practical Evaluation Portfolio journal on Moodle.

Other session sign-offs (portfolio evidence, not separate Moodle assignments)

These sessions have no Moodle assignment of their own. Sign them off in the session and check the portfolio entry. They count toward consistency, documentation and problem solving.

For every session, tick all three before signing:

Works (deliverable shown working on device),

Portfolio (entry recorded),

Commit (project files committed to the student’s repository).
